Privacy Policy

FoodLens respects your privacy and follows applicable privacy laws. This policy explains what information the service collects, why it is used, where it is stored, and what rights and choices you have.

1. Data Controller

  • Operating entity: FoodLens Team
  • Contact email: support@foodlens.com

2. Information We Collect

The service may process the information below depending on the features you choose to use.

2.1 Account and Authentication Information

  • Email address, password hash, verification status, and reset status when you sign in with email
  • Provider identifier, email address, display name, and related login information when you sign in with Google or Kakao
  • Session information, refresh tokens, device identifiers, and request IDs used for request tracing

2.2 Profile and Personalization Information

  • Display name
  • Allergy information, dietary restrictions, and severity settings
  • Language and traveler card settings
  • Optional information such as gender, birth year, disliked ingredients, current travel location, or coordinates

2.3 Content You Upload or Create

  • Images used for label, food, or barcode analysis
  • Profile images
  • Analysis results, history, and saved records
  • Misanalysis reports or support inquiries you submit

2.4 Automatically Collected Information

  • App version, OS version, device model, usage records, and access logs
  • Diagnostic information used for error tracking and incident response
  • The current app does not include an advertising SDK and does not collect device information or advertising IDs, including IDFA or AAID, for advertising delivery or measurement

2.5 Optional Permission Information

  • Camera: taking photos of food, labels, or barcodes
  • Photo library: selecting and saving images
  • Location: providing country or region based food safety information and travel context

These permissions are used only when you grant them.

3. How We Use Personal Information

FoodLens uses collected information for the following purposes.

  • Providing food, label, and barcode analysis results
  • Providing allergy-based warnings and personalized results
  • Providing history, profile, settings, and traveler card features
  • Login, session maintenance, account security, password reset, and email verification
  • Responding to support inquiries and misanalysis reports
  • Improving service quality, responding to incidents, and preventing abuse
  • Meeting legal obligations and handling disputes

5. Retention Period

FoodLens generally deletes personal information without delay after the purpose of processing is achieved, but may retain information for limited periods when needed for operations, security, or legal obligations.

  • Account, profile, allergy, settings, and history data: until you delete the data or close your account
  • Original uploaded media: default 30 days
  • Derived media and rendered results: default 90 days
  • Operational logs: default 14 days
  • Records related to authentication, password reset, or deletion requests may be retained separately for the period needed for security and operations

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights and choices.

  • View and edit your profile, allergy information, settings, and history
  • Revoke app permissions
  • Contact FoodLens about personal information processing
  • Request Delete My Data
    This keeps the account and resets saved profile, history, personalization data, and media.
  • Request Delete Account
    This deletes the account and related data and invalidates sessions.

7. Safety and Limits

  • FoodLens is an assistive tool for food safety decisions and does not replace medical diagnosis or professional nutrition advice.
  • AI analysis results may not be 100% accurate. If you have severe allergies or health concerns, use FoodLens together with professional judgment.

8. International Transfers

Some processors used by the service, including Google, Render, SendGrid, and Sentry, may process data outside Korea. FoodLens uses these services with appropriate safeguards where required.

9. Contact

For questions about personal information processing, contact us at:

  • Email: support@foodlens.com

10. Changes

This policy may be updated because of changes to laws or the service. If there are important changes, FoodLens will notify users through the app or documents.
